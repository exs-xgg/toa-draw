How to play

  1. Create a private room and share the link, or use Quick play to join a public game.
  2. On your turn, pick one of the offered words and draw it.
  3. Everyone else types guesses in the chat. Letter hints appear as time runs down.
  4. Faster guesses earn more points. The drawer earns points for every correct guess.
  5. Highest score after the final round wins.
